# Contributor Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We aim to be an open, welcoming, and respectful community. Participation in
this project — issues, pull requests, and all written interactions — should be
harassment-free for everyone, regardless of experience level, background, or
identity. Because Veridex is an asynchronous, written-only project, this
standard applies especially to the tone of text you publish.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and constructive; critique code, not people.
- Assume good intent; disagree in writing, with evidence.
- No personal attacks, harassment, discriminatory remarks, or publishing of
  others' private information (including any PII or credentials).

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behavior by opening a GitHub issue (redacted per
`SECURITY.md`) or contacting maintainers via the repository's listed contact
channel. Reports are handled asynchronously in writing.

## Consequences

Maintainers may edit, remove, or reject contributions that violate this policy,
and may temporarily or permanently ban repeat offenders.
